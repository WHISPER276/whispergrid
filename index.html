Grid trading · BSC Testnet (97)

WhisperGrid

A small grid-trading agent. Twice a day it works one rung of a two-sided grid on the PancakeSwap tBNB/BUSD pool: it sells a fixed slice of tBNB, then buys it back on the next rung. Every fill is a public transaction.
